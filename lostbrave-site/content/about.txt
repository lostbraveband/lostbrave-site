Lost Brave is a five-piece original band from Milwaukee, rooted somewhere between the roadhouse and the front porch: rock, blues, folk and Americana, with violin, keys and slide wound through layered guitars and three voices that lean into each other.

Every song is an original. Each member writes their own parts, and the arrangements grow out of the room: built to be played live, and loud when they need to be.

In 2026 the band took the WAMI Stage at Summerfest and packed Linneman's Riverwest Inn. Now they're recording their first EP themselves, keeping it raw and close to the sound you hear at a show.
