Lost Brave is a five-piece Milwaukee band playing original roots rock and Americana: driving guitars, violin, keys and slide over a blues-and-folk foundation, with three voices stacked on top. The set runs hard from first song to last, with a few quieter moments where the strings and harmonies come forward.

The band formed in late 2025, but the players aren't new to the stage. Its members have played together since 2022 in Petty Cash, a Milwaukee-area Tom Petty cover band whose Wisconsin State Fair set on the Leff's Lucky Town Miller Lite Stage drew several hundred people on a Friday afternoon.

Lost Brave played its first show at MUSIC6S in December 2025. Seven months later it played Summerfest's WAMI / MUSIC6S stage, drawing more than 75 fans on a Thursday afternoon in 93-degree heat. In November 2026 it helped draw 113 people to a shared bill at Linneman's Riverwest Inn.

Sets run 60 to 90 minutes, all original, drawn from a catalog of about 50 songs. The band brings its own amps and drums and is happy to share backline. Its first singles arrive this winter, with a full-length album to follow.
